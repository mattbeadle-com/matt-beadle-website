A few useful answers.

Can you host a whole event in English or German?

Yes. I work in native English and fluent German, including events where the programme moves between both languages. We will agree the audience journey and language transitions during the briefing.

Can you help shape the programme?

Yes. I can be involved during planning, helping you think through the flow, guest conversations and audience involvement, as well as delivering the event itself.

What do you need for an initial enquiry?

A date or date range, location, event format, audience and a short description of what you want to achieve. If the programme is still taking shape, that is a good time to talk.

How do you prepare for an event?

Preparation starts with your objectives, audience and key messages. We work through the running order, speakers, names and pronunciations, then agree introductions, transitions and questions. The aim is to make the live event feel natural because the groundwork is clear.

Can you moderate panels and interview senior leaders?

Yes. I help turn a topic into a focused conversation, with questions that draw out different perspectives and make the subject relevant to the audience. We can agree the key themes in advance while leaving room for follow-up questions and spontaneous moments.

Do you host live streams and hybrid events?

Yes. I present live broadcasts, streams and hybrid formats as well as events in the room. The briefing covers both audiences: how we address the camera, bring remote guests into the conversation and keep the programme connected across screens and stage.

Do you work internationally?

Yes. I am based in Germany and work internationally. My work has taken me to over 20 countries, with audiences representing 40 nationalities and more than 30,000 people to date. Share your location, dates and programme when you enquire so we can discuss travel, preparation and the event schedule together.

Can you work with technical or specialist subjects?

Yes. My job is to ask informed questions and help the audience follow the conversation. Briefing materials and time with your subject experts help me understand the terminology, the important distinctions and what your audience needs to take away.

Can the audience get involved?

Absolutely. Depending on the format, we can build in audience questions, short conversations, polls or other moments of participation. The choice should serve the event and feel comfortable for the people in the room.

What happens if the programme changes on the day?

Live events need flexibility. I stay close to the production team, listen to what is happening and help keep the audience oriented when timings or plans shift. Clear cues and an agreed way to communicate backstage make that much easier.

How do we arrange rehearsals and technical requirements?

We agree the practical details with your event and production teams: microphones, stage layout, slides, camera positions, cues and any rehearsal time. Please flag live links, demonstrations or other complex moments early so we can plan them properly.

How much does moderation cost?

The fee depends on the format, duration, preparation, location and any additional filming or usage requirements. Send me a short brief and I can put together a proposal for your event.
