About Matt Beadle

Curious about people.
At home in a live moment.

I’m Matt Beadle, a British event moderator, presenter, keynote speaker and author based near Bielefeld, Germany. I have called Germany home since 2000. My work has taken me from leadership workshops and corporate productions to international stages and professional football.

My starting point

Family first.
Words and music.

My family is at the centre of everything I do. They are my starting point, whatever stage I happen to be standing on.

My love of words and rhetoric comes from my mother, a linguist. My love of the stage comes from my father, a musician. Between them, they gave me a fascination with language and the pleasure of bringing it to life in front of an audience.

I followed those interests through a Bachelor’s degree in Sport Science and a Master’s in Linguistics. Today, they meet in my work as a host, speaker and football leader: helping people connect, understand one another and perform at their best.

Even the surname seems to fit. Historically, a beadle could act as a town crier — bringing people together and making himself heard. Funny that I seem to be living up to the name. Nominative determinism, perhaps?

I work in native English and fluent German. British humour tends to come along for the ride.

What it is like to work together

Thoughtful preparation.
A natural presence.

I like to understand the brief, the audience and what matters to the team behind the event. From there, we can build a clear shape and leave space for the human moments that make it memorable.

For an initial conversation, you do not need to have every detail worked out. A sense of purpose is a good place to begin.
