Technical Director · SC Verl

Connecting the football,
from principle to practice.

As Technical Director at SC Verl, I am part of the leadership group shaping the club’s football identity and long-term sporting strategy.

My role brings together recruitment, academy development and first-team philosophy. The aim is a coherent approach: decisions about players, coaches and development should connect with the football the club wants to play.

For me, this work starts with clarity. What makes us distinctive? Which principles guide our decisions? How do we give talented people a meaningful route to progress?

Those questions matter beyond an individual match. They help a club hold its direction while continuing to learn and adapt.

The work behind the result

One club.
A connected approach.

Identity, recruitment, development and culture need to support one another. Each decision contributes to the environment in which football happens.

01 / Football identity

Know what you stand for.

Help define a clear playing DNA and the principles behind it. A shared football identity gives the club a reference point for its sporting decisions, across different teams and stages of development.

02 / Recruitment

Find the right fit.

Connect recruitment with the playing philosophy and the club’s wider direction. Talent matters, alongside how a player can contribute to the team, develop within the environment and fit the football we want to play.

03 / Player pathways

Make development a journey.

Link academy development with the demands of senior football. My earlier work as Head of Performance included building the club’s player pathway model; that longer view continues to inform how I think about opportunity and progression.

04 / Leadership & culture

Keep the direction shared.

Bring the football strategy, the people and the daily environment into alignment. Clear expectations, shared responsibility and a consistent development philosophy help connect individual work with the club’s purpose.
